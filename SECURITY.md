# Security

This app holds the names, email addresses, phone numbers and photos of people who drop
off ceramics at Asmbly Makerspace. If you find a way to see someone else's details or
photos, to get into the staff console without being ceramics staff, to send email
through the app, or anything else that looks like a security problem, please tell us
privately.

## How to report

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: on this repository's **Security** tab,
choose **Report a vulnerability**. Only the maintainers see the report.

Please don't open a public issue or pull request for it, and don't test against the live
app with real people's data or by sending email to anyone but yourself.

Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it lets someone do. We'll reply on the
report.
